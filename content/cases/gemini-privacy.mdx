<TLDR>
- **Problem** Since Personal Intelligence (Jan 2026), Gemini can read your connected Gmail, Calendar, Photos, YouTube and Search history to personalize answers. But it's hard to see which detail came from where, the switches sit in two places, and a deletion can't be checked.
- **Solution** Move control to the answer itself: see the source, switch it off in place, and check the result.
- **Impact** In this concept, one toggle under the answer replaces two settings trips, and a deletion you can check replaces a promise. The usability test will measure whether that builds trust.
</TLDR>
