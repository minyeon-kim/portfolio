<TLDR>
- **Problem:** Gemini can pull Gmail, Calendar and Photos into an answer, but people can't see where a detail came from, and turning it off means digging through two settings pages.
- **My role:** Research with one teammate, then solo from problem definition to a coded prototype and usability test.
- **Outcome:** A working prototype that shows the source under the answer, turns it off in place, and lets people check that a deletion worked. Usability test in progress.
</TLDR>

<CaseSection id="overview" label="01 / Overview" title="Connecting took one tap. | Cleaning up took a trip to settings.">

You ask Gemini for an Italian restaurant, and it adds: “You loved the truffle risotto when you went with Jeongmin last spring.” You never told it that name. Since [Personal Intelligence](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/personal-intelligence/) linked Gmail, Calendar and Photos (January 2026), Gemini can bring up things you never asked about, and turning them off means digging through two settings pages.

[Try the prototype](https://minyeon-kim.github.io/gemini_privacy/?mode=participant) · A self-initiated concept, not affiliated with Google.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection id="discover-primary" label="05 / Discover · Interviews" title="Worried, but still using it.">

We interviewed a call-center worker and an everyday AI user, and answered the same guide ourselves, as a designer and a security professional, to test it. Everyone kept using AI because it was convenient, but turned off integrations when trust broke. The call-center worker, who asks people for personal data, said they expect a deletion request to erase everything.

> “Even when I delete it, I don't know if the record is really deleted.”

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection id="define" label="06 / Define" title="Anxiety starts when your data shows up, | not when you type it.">

<CaseFindings
  title="Two decisions behind the screens"
  items={[
    { label: "Decision 1", title: "Show what each source allows", body: "Memory can be deleted; Gmail and Calendar can only be blocked, and the originals stay where they live. The screen's structure says so, instead of hiding the difference." },

<CaseSection id="deliver" label="08 / Deliver" title="A prototype you can actually click, built to be tested.">

Built in HTML and JavaScript with a moderator and a participant mode. It logs clicks, misclicks and time to find the Source badge, so every session turns into behavioral data.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection id="prototype" label="09 / Final prototype" title="Check it, turn it off, right where the answer is.">

**Scenario B.** “Delete everything about Jeongmin.” What actually goes away, and how do I know it did?

</CaseSection>
