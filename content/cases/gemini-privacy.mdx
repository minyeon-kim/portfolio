<DevsignCase study={study}>
<TLDR>
- **Problem** Since Personal Intelligence (Jan 2026), Gemini reads your Gmail, Calendar, Photos, YouTube and Search history to personalize answers, even unasked. But the answer doesn't say where a detail came from, the switches sit in two places, and a deletion can't be checked.
- **Solution** Move control to the answer itself: see the source, switch it off in place, and check the result.
- **Impact** Two settings trips became one toggle under the answer, and a promise of deletion became a result you can check. The usability test will measure whether that builds trust.
</TLDR>
</DevsignCase>
