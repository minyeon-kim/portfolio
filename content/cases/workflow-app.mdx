<TLDR>
- **Problem** Designers and developers both use AI to make things now, but the drafts and the reasons behind them end up in different tools. Teams still pick a version in chat, and differences between the design and the built product show up only after release.
- **Solution** One workspace that takes a team from comparing drafts to a recorded decision. Drafts are combined area by area, every difference between design and build goes to one decision-maker, and each decision is saved with the screen it changed.
- **Impact** Decisions that used to get lost in chat now follow one path you can trace back. In testing, the designer and the developer both rated it 5 out of 5 for making design–code differences and the reasons for changes easier to find.
