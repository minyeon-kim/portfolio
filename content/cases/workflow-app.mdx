<TLDR>
- **Problem** Designers and developers both use AI, but drafts and their reasons end up in different tools. Teams pick versions in chat, and mismatches show up after release.
- **Solution** One workspace from comparing drafts to a recorded decision: combine drafts by area, send each mismatch to one decision-maker, and save each decision with its screen.
- **Impact** With 2 testers, first tries needed more help than planned (2.3, goal 1.5), though both saw the value. We changed the screens twice. It’s still a prototype, and the new screens are not retested yet.
