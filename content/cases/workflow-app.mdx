<DevsignCase study={study}>
<TLDR>
- **Problem** Designers and developers both build with AI now, but the drafts and the reasons behind them end up in different tools. Teams still settle on one version in chat, and gaps between design and build show up after release.
- **Solution** One workspace that takes a team from comparing drafts to a recorded decision. Drafts merge area by area, each build gap goes to one person who decides, and every decision stays with its screen.
- **Impact** Decisions that used to scatter across chat now follow one path you can trace. In testing, both participants expected design–code gaps and the reasons for changes to be easier to find (5.0/5).
</TLDR>
</DevsignCase>
