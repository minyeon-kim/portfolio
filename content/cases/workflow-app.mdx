<DevsignCase study={study}>
<TLDR>
- **Problem** Both roles build with AI, but results and the reasons behind them are scattered across tools — so agreeing on one version still happens over chat, and design–build gaps surface only after release.
- **Solution** One workspace from comparing drafts to a recorded decision: merge drafts by area, send build gaps to one decision-maker, and keep every decision with its screen.
- **Impact** Scattered chat decisions became one traceable flow — in testing, both roles expected finding design–code gaps and tracing changes to get easier (5.0/5).
</TLDR>
