<TLDR>
- **Problem** Designers and developers both use AI, but drafts and their reasons end up in different tools. Teams choose a version in chat, and differences from the design show up only after release.
- **Solution** One workspace from comparing drafts to a recorded decision: combine drafts by area, send each mismatch to one decision-maker, and save each decision with its screen.
- **Impact** Both testers gave 5 out of 5 to “we’ll ask less often why design and code differ.”
</TLDR>
